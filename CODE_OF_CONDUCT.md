# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Specifically:

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Critique code, not people. "This breaks X" beats "you broke X", and both beat
  anything about the person who wrote it.
- Answer questions you have time for, and say nothing rather than something
  dismissive when you don't.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or bringing someone's identity into a
  technical discussion. Ever.

If someone is making this project worse to participate in, report it to
j@soundsys.co. Reports stay private. Maintainers may edit, lock or delete
contributions, and ban accounts, for behaviour that breaks the above.
